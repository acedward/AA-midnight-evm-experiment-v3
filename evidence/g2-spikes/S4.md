Maker (OwnerA) fee capacity before → after: NIGHT 400000000000 → 400000000000, NIGHT UTXOs registered for dust generation 1 → 1.

The maker is funded and DUST-registered **on purpose**: "the maker paid no fees" is only
worth asserting about a wallet that could have paid. The decisive evidence is not a balance
but the settled transaction's own intents — fees are dust actions, dust actions belong to an
intent, and the maker's intent is the one carrying the contract call. Spike S6 develops that
reading in full. (The dust BALANCE is deliberately not leaned on: the pinned facade's dust
accessors read 0 even for a wallet that is actively paying fees — 00005's own wallet report
printed `DUST=0` for the genesis wallet while it was funding the whole run.)
