## What this proves

1. The pinned wallet SDK (`@midnightntwrk/wallet-sdk` 2.0.0-beta.2) **builds, signs, proves and
   submits real transactions** against node `2.0.0-rc.4` + indexer `4.4.0-rc.1` + proof server
   `9.0.0-rc.3`, on a fresh `undeployed1` ledger-9 network.
   Flow used (the pinned SDK's own active e2e pattern):
   `transferTransaction -> signRecipe -> finalizeRecipe -> submitTransaction`.
2. **DUST generation works**: NIGHT UTXOs registered via
   `registerNightUtxosForDustGeneration` flip to `registeredForDustGeneration: true`, and a
   subsequent transaction's fees are payable from the generated DUST — the smoke transfer
   succeeded with no other fee source.
3. **Fee isolation holds**: the fee payer is a distinct wallet from every party whose demo-color
   balances are under test; NIGHT/DUST never appear in the demo balance tables.

## Note on DUST balance reads (not a defect)

`state.dust.balance` reads `undefined` because DUST accrues continuously and the balance is a
function of time (`capabilities.coinsAndBalances.getWalletBalance(now)` /
`dust.state.state.walletBalance(now)`). The harness therefore asserts DUST capability by the
observable facts above — registration flag flipping and fees actually being paid — rather than by
a single balance read. The SDK's own shortfall errors (`have N, need M`) and
`waitForGeneratedDust(utxos, amount)` are used to wait deterministically instead of sleeping.
