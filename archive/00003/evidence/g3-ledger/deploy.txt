## Finding G3-1 — RESOLVED (harness defect, not a lane defect)

`deployContract` was being called with the pre-beta.6 option shape. midnight-js
`v5.0.0-beta.6` does **not** accept a raw `new Contract(witnesses)`:
`DeployContractOptions.compiledContract` is a compact-js `CompiledContract`, constructed as

    CompiledContract.make(tag, Ctor)
      |> withWitnesses({...}) | withVacantWitnesses     // Minter declares none
      |> withCompiledFileAssets(<zk artifacts dir>)

Passing the raw class left the witness context unset, which is precisely what
`compact-js`'s `getContractContext` dereferenced
(`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'Symbol()')`).

**This was a defect in this harness, NOT an incoherence in the pinned lane.** No lane RED is
recorded, and the SDK-level path stands.

Three further real beta.6 requirements surfaced on the way to a successful deploy:

1. `compact-js` is a transitive dependency; it must be imported from the supported re-export
   subpath `@midnight-ntwrk/midnight-js-protocol/compact-js/effect`.
2. `levelPrivateStateProvider` requires a `privateStoragePasswordProvider` **and** an `accountId`.
3. That password is validated: it must contain at least 3 of {uppercase, lowercase, digits,
   special}. A bare hex string is rejected. The harness generates an ephemeral per-process
   password meeting the rule; it is never written to disk or evidence.
